Hello, this is Ratul,

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Port City International University (PCIU), Chattogram, Bangladesh. My research interests span machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, digital image processing, multimodal learning and renewable energy. My work includes solar irradiance forecasting, Bengali text classification, explainable AI for plant disease detection, medical audio analysis, and efficient language models.

At PCIU, I teach Artificial Intelligence, Pattern Recognition, Data Structure, Structured Programming Language, Discrete Mathematics, and Mathematical and Probabilistic Analysis, and I supervise undergraduate thesis and project works. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from PCIU in February 2022. Before joining PCIU as a Lecturer in July 2023, I worked as a Research Assistant in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) and as a Computer Instructor at MAS Marine Academy.

Research Interests

  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning — time-series forecasting, classification, and explainable AI (XAI) for applied problems.
  • Natural Language Processing — Bengali emotion classification and hate speech categorization, hallucination reduction in large language models, and efficient multilingual Text-to-SQL with small language models.
  • Computer Vision & Digital Image Processing — plant disease detection and image-based classification.
  • Multimodal Learning — unified architectures for multidimensional human emotion recognition.
  • Renewable Energy — solar irradiance forecasting, wind–solar hybrid grids, and power system analysis.
